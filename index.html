Our Services

Compassionate, confidential mental health support for Chico State students.

What We Offer

We are here to support Chico State students with their mental health needs. Individual counseling is available in-person and via Zoom. Group counseling is offered in-person to foster connection and shared experiences. Our dedicated team is committed to helping you thrive during your time at Chico State.

Individual Counseling

One-on-one sessions available in-person at our office or via Zoom for your convenience. Our licensed counselors provide a safe, confidential space for you to explore your concerns.

Group Counseling

In-person group sessions foster connection and shared experiences. Groups offer a supportive environment where students can learn from each other and develop coping strategies together.

Let's Chat! Drop-In

No appointment necessary. Stop by the WellCat Counseling Center during office hours to speak with a counselor. We're here to listen.

Our Commitment to Diversity

At the WellCat Counseling Center at Chico State, we value diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment that values and respects all members of our campus community. We recognize that each student brings unique cultural backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives. Our counselors strive to provide culturally responsive services and to foster a welcoming space where every student feels seen, heard, and supported regardless of race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, religion, socioeconomic status, or any other aspect of identity.

⚑ Crisis Support

You are not alone. Help is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

If this is a life-threatening emergency, call 9-1-1 or go to the nearest emergency room.

24-Hour Crisis Resources

If you or someone you know is in emotional distress or experiencing a mental health crisis, please reach out to any of the following resources:

  • Mental Health Crisis — Emergency In a life threatening emergency, call 9-1-1 or go directly to the nearest emergency room. 9-1-1
  • National Suicide Prevention Lifeline 1-800-273-8255
  • ProtoCall Crisis Line Available 24/7 through the WellCat Counseling Center 530-898-6345, press option 2
  • 24-Hour Crisis Line 855-530-6802
  • Butte County Behavioral Health (Chico area) 1-800-334-6622 or 530-891-2810
  • The Trevor Project — LGBTQ+ Line 1-866-488-7386

What to Expect When You Call

During Office Hours

Call 530-898-6345 during office hours to speak directly with a counselor. If you are in immediate distress, let the staff know and you will be prioritized.

After Hours

Call 855-530-6802 or 530-898-6345 and press option 2 to reach the ProtoCall crisis line. A trained crisis counselor is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Worried About a Friend?

If you are concerned about someone, trust your instincts. You can:

  • Talk to them directly and listen without judgment.
  • Call any of the crisis lines listed above for guidance.
  • Contact the WellCat Counseling Center to consult with a counselor.
  • Report a concern through the university's reporting system.

UMatter

Outreach. Awareness. Positive mental health.

About UMatter

UMatter is the outreach arm of the counseling center, designed to eliminate negative stigmas and promote healthy student interaction and positive mental health practices.

Our Mission

We work to break down barriers to mental health care by creating engaging campus events, peer education programs, and awareness campaigns that reach students where they are.

🎙️ Chatter with UMatter Podcast

Listen to our podcast, Chatter with UMatter, where we discuss student life, mental health topics, coping strategies, and feature conversations with campus and community members.

Land Acknowledgment

The California State University, Chico campus sits on the lands of the Mechoopda people. We recognize their distinctive spiritual relationship with this land and the waters that run through campus. We are humbled that our campus resides upon sacred lands that once combated combatted combated sustained the Mechoopda people for centuries. We recognize the Mechoopda people as the traditional custodians of this land and acknowledge their continuing connection to the land, waters, and culture. We pay our respects to the Mechoopda people and their elders, past, present, and emerging.
